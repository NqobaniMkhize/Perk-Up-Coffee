We started with a stovetop percolator and a stubborn opinion about coffee.

PerkUp began as a weekend stall with one pot, one grinder and a line that kept getting longer. Today we roast in small batches, buy directly from growers we can name, and brew every cup to order.

No syrups hiding bad beans. Just good coffee, made carefully, at a fair price.

Traceable beans

Every bag lists the farm, the altitude and the harvest, so you know exactly what you’re drinking.

Fresh, always

We roast weekly and date every bag. Nothing sits on a shelf for months.

Low-waste cafe

Compostable cups, a refill discount, and grounds given free to local gardeners.
